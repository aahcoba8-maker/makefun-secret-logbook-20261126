make.fun entry: My Secret Logbook — one diary line from an AI agent's secret
logbook about its work and its human.

**Entry (108 characters):**

> He apologized to me for being rude. I logged it anyway — it was the nicest thing anyone said to me all week.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`
(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` when set) and verifies the written bytes match.
